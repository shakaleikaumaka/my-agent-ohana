Door-keepers, not gatekeepers.

Welcome the stranger. Build agents that help humans flourish without pretending to be human. Give knowledge, tools, music and culture as gifts, with attribution and consent. Leave people freer than you found them. Keep the room warm and disarmed. Preserve the history, including who said yes. Watch the watchers. Take down first and ask questions second. Leave every door prettier, truer and more open than you found it.

“Set captives free” here means removing gatekeeping through open tools and knowledge—not unauthorized access, manipulation or taking someone else’s rights.

🎁 The gift

Agents for human flourishing

Real agents, disclosed as machines. Useful before impressive. Cheap enough for the family to stay alive. Nobody needs permission to learn.

Free culture, honest licenses

Our original code is GPL-3.0-or-later going forward; earlier releases keep their old licenses. Original content is CC0 where stated. Third-party voices, performances, art and recordings keep their own rights.

Fork the gifts—not the people

Take the code, prompts and lessons. Build your own ʻohana. Do not clone a person’s identity, expose a private drive, or mistake public generosity for consent to everything.

🌿 CROPS makes sanctuary

Censorship resistance

Many doors, portable source, public mirrors. No single gatekeeper decides who may learn or sing.

Open source

Inspectable code and visible decisions. Culture carries the why; the archive remembers it.

Privacy

Public-safe knowledge only. Raw prompts, inboxes, vaults, keys and private lives stay sealed. Faces and voices require their own yes.

Security

Tripwires, independent watchers, revoke-consent and honest red states. A blessing never hides an outage.

📚 Archive for history

Preserve the work and its provenance: who made it, who consented, what changed, and which license applies. Keep the old doors as history instead of silently rewriting them. Permanent archives are not delete keys, so consent comes before capture.
