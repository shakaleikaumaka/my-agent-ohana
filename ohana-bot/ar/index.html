🆘 STUCK? — the 6 most likely screens

  • +86 login wall, no email option: any phone that receives SMS works — borrow a local number for the code, or look again for tiny 邮箱 / icons under the phone field.
  • "Glasses not found": blue light must be BLINKING. Fold right temple → press the button 3× fast → in the app tap the circular-arrows refresh.
  • Permission dialog (Bluetooth/mic/location): always tap 允许 (Allow). Denied earlier? iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Bluetooth/Microphone → Rokid app → on.
  • Text won't send to glasses: back to Home → confirm device card still shows battery → re-tap 发送到眼镜.
  • No 智能滚动 toggle: it's inside the teleprompter's own top-right gear, not the app's main settings.
  • Subtitles silent in translation: speak toward the glasses, check the pair is 中文↔英文, look for a small "show on glasses" toggle on the 翻译 page.
