✨ The glow & the touch MUST

WS2812B 灯带 1米 60灯 ×2（5V，IP30 不防水，好剪好焊）
WS2812B LED strip, 1m 60-LED ×2 (5V, IP30 non-waterproof)
This is the six glowing faces. One addressable RGB LED chain, ONE data wire, software lights any pixel any colour — 12 px per face × 6 faces = 72 px, so ×2 is required maths, not a spare (one 60-LED metre is 12 px short). Cut into six 12-px segments, chain up the spine: speaker's face solid-bright, others at 5%, idle = slow breathing through all six. Highest charisma per ¥ in the build.
Better if the stall has them: WS2812B 12位 RGB 灯环 ×6 (12-LED rings) = exactly 72 px, no cutting or re-soldering, and round like a face. Rings win on time, strips win on price.

💡 Market tips

  • Test before paying: ask the stall to power the Pi (they all have cables) — dead boards get sold to foreigners.
  • One stall, whole basket: module stalls carry LED strips + TTP223 + wires + switches; bundle for the discount.
  • Pay by Alipay/WeChat — cash sometimes "has no change."
  • Keep receipts/photos of every purchase — for the sponsor and for ShanHaiWoo reimbursement if the kit ask lands retroactively.
  • Skip-list if squeezed: servo → acrylic (use cardboard + paper) → second LED strip → ReSpeaker (USB puck fine).
